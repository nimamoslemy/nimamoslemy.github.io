PROFILE & RESEARCH VISION

About Me

Edinburgh, UK Additive Manufacturing & AI

Greetings — I am Nima Moslemy, a PhD Researcher in Mechanical Engineering and Additive Manufacturing at Heriot-Watt University, working on an EPSRC-funded research programme.

My work bridges physical engineering and intelligent software: combining Laser Powder Bed Fusion (LPBF), architected lattice metamaterials, and finite element simulation (FEA) with machine learning for in-situ defect detection and high-performance desktop AI applications.

Research Interests

  • Metal Additive Manufacturing (LPBF / SLM)
  • Architected Lattice Structures & Metamaterials
  • Finite Element Analysis & Multi-Physics Simulation
  • Biomedical Engineering & 3D Bioprinting
  • Patient-Specific Implants & Prostheses

Core Expertise

  • AI & Deep Learning for Defect Detection
  • Native Systems & AI Software (Python, C++20, C#)
  • Mechanical & Biomechanical Characterization
  • CAD / Implicit Modeling (nTopology, SolidWorks)
  • Medical Image Processing (Materialise Mimics/Magics)
